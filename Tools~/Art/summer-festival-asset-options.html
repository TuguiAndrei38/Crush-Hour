04 / Before implementation

Fit the assets to Crush Hour

Crowd and staff

Keep the installed David Jalbert crowd and its current animation setup. Use matching bodies for staff with custom high-visibility vests, navy uniforms, caps and megaphones. Pack characters are optional and are not a crowd replacement recommendation.

Effects and ground

Preserve the gas visibility and danger overlays. Fire, smoke and gas must stay readable at the gameplay zoom. New models replace visuals; their detailed meshes should not silently change the grid simulation.
